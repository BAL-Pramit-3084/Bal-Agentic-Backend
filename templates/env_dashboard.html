Recommendation: Investigate direct emission sources in furnace operations for optimization opportunities.
🔴 GCP Dust Generation Spike
October 2025 shows abnormal GCP dust generation of 11,866 MT against production of 17,599 MT (67.4% ratio). Historical comparison: September showed similar anomaly at 11,647 MT. Normal range (Apr-Aug 2025) averaged 236 MT. This 50x increase indicates either: (1) Measurement unit change in recording, (2) Process change generating more dust, or (3) Data entry error. Immediate Action: Verify data accuracy with Production and Environment teams before next regulatory filing.
